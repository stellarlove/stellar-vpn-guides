# Which sites and apps need a VPN in China: the 2026 list

**In short: anything whose account lives with Google, Meta, OpenAI or Anthropic needs an overseas route from China; Chinese services and most static websites do not.** Here is the everyday breakdown so you know what to prepare before you arrive.

## Needs Stellar

| Category | Services |
| --- | --- |
| Search and mail | Google Search, Gmail, Google Drive / Docs / Photos, Google Maps, Google Scholar |
| Video and music | YouTube, Netflix, Disney+, Spotify, Twitch |
| Social | Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X, Reddit, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, Signal |
| AI tools | ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Midjourney, Claude Code, Codex CLI |
| Work | Slack, Notion, Figma, Dropbox, some Zoom setups |
| Development | GitHub (intermittent), official npm / PyPI registries, Docker Hub, Hugging Face |
| Academic | most overseas journal databases, arXiv is sometimes slow, university VPN portals |
| News | most international media |

## Works directly

- WeChat, Alipay, Taobao, JD, Meituan, Didi, 12306, Chinese banking apps
- Chinese video and music: Bilibili, iQIYI, NetEase Music, QQ Music
- China sites of most global brands (Apple, Microsoft China)
- iCloud China, the Chinese App Store

## In between

- **Apple**: an overseas Apple ID's App Store usually opens but downloads slowly; iMessage and FaceTime work.
- **Microsoft**: Outlook.com, OneDrive and Teams are inconsistent; stable with Stellar.
- **Steam**: the store is slow, downloads via CDN are usually fine.
- **Zoom / Google Meet**: international Zoom calls may need the route, Google Meet does.

## The easy way

Keep Stellar in **smart mode**: the first table goes through the overseas route automatically, the second stays direct, no switching. If a site complains about your region, switch to **global mode** for a moment. See [modes](https://stellar.dog/en/docs/modes).

Install and log in before you fly; after landing just open the app. Full checklist in [returning to China](https://stellar.dog/en/docs/returning-to-china).
